# Varnika-GlowBot

Your AI-powered skincare and wellness companion that scans your face to deliver personalized natural remedies and targeted yoga routines for a radiant, healthy glow.

---

## Key Features

* **AI Facial Recognition :** Scans unique skin texture, contours, and tone to analyze specific skin needs instantly.
* **Personalized Home Remedies :** Matches skin profiles with tailored natural solutions.
* **Targeted Face Yoga :** Generates custom facial exercise routines to boost circulation, tone muscles, and naturally firm skin.
* **Daily Tracking :** Monitors skin progress over time to adjust routines dynamically as the complexion improves.
