# Varnika-GlowBot
Your AI-powered skincare and wellness companion that scans your face to deliver personalized natural remedies and targeted yoga routines for a radiant, healthy glow.


## Key Features

### AI Facial Recognition: Scans your unique skin texture, contours, and tone to analyze your specific skin needs instantly.
### Hyper-Personalized Remedies: Matches your skin profile with tailored, chemical-free herbal and natural solutions.
### Targeted Face Yoga: Generates custom facial exercise routines to boost circulation, tone muscles, and naturally firm your skin.
### Holistic Tracking: Monitors your skin’s progress over time to adjust routines as your complexion improves.
